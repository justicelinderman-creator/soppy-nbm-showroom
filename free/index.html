How to use this checklist

A CLEAR WEBSITE ISN'T ENOUGH.

A customer needs to understand your services, use the page on a phone, and reach the right person. This free self-audit is designed for detailers, cleaners, roofers, repair companies, and other local service businesses. It measures visible usability issues, not actual conversion performance.

Test with your phone

Open your own website on a small screen. Try its navigation, telephone link, service descriptions, and quote form. Don't assume a form works simply because the submit button looks correct.

Verify delivery, not just design

Send an authorized test inquiry through your actual business website. Confirm it arrives in the correct inbox, check spam, and verify the customer sees an accurate confirmation message.

Fix the highest friction first

Wrong contact details, inaccessible controls, and undelivered inquiries deserve attention before cosmetic changes or paid advertising. Re-test after every repair.

Keep every claim accurate

Use approved service areas, genuine reviews, and clear estimates. A checklist cannot guarantee more customers, higher rankings, or increased revenue.
