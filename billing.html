Cosie Originals provides scoped website, automation, and other digital production services. Every paid engagement begins with a written scope and agreed payment terms. This website is a portfolio and inquiry destination, not a checkout.

Before a payment

We confirm the deliverables, exclusions, price in USD, required client materials, delivery timing, acceptance criteria, included revisions, ownership or license terms, and any third-party costs in writing. A public service price describes a standard scope; a project begins only after the particular scope is accepted. Payment requests are made separately using an agreed invoice or hosted payment link when our provider is available. The site does not collect card numbers.

Website and workflow pilots

The standard one-page website pilot is quoted at $750, usually split into a $375 start payment and $375 after the agreed deliverable is accepted. Other services are quoted individually.

Cancellation

You can request cancellation by emailing us at any time. If you cancel before we begin work, any advance payment for that project is fully refundable. If work has begun, we will identify the agreed work already completed and provide a fair refund of payments for work not yet delivered or performed. We will explain the proposed accounting in writing. A change to scope, timeline, or price requires written agreement.

Refunds and delivery problems

If we cannot complete the agreed work, we will offer to fix the issue or arrange a refund for the undelivered portion; if none of the agreed work can be delivered, we will refund the amounts paid for that work. For delivered work, tell us what fails the agreed acceptance criteria so we can address it within the revision and correction terms in the signed scope. Approved, completed work is generally not automatically refundable solely because a customer's plans change. This does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.

Refund requests are reviewed against the written scope, work actually delivered, and amounts paid. Approved card refunds return through the original payment method where supported; your payment provider or bank determines when the funds are visible. We do not ask customers to send card details or passwords by email.
