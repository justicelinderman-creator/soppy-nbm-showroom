# NBM Sales Funnel v1 — Review Candidate

**Status:** REVIEW ONLY. No public customer intake, payment, deployment approval, or outbound marketing authorized by this document.

## Goal

Turn the existing NBM showroom into a truthful, usable path from interested service business to a scoped conversation and — only after approval — a written agreement and payment. The first objective is one real paid pilot, not an invented conversion statistic.

## Current verified baseline (2026-11-02 audit)

- Public static showroom, fictional DETAILFLOW demo, and browser-only 12-point free audit exist.
- The Studio page advertises a proposed **$750** fixed-scope website + quote-flow pilot.
- Main showroom pages intentionally use `noindex,nofollow`; the free audit is indexable.
- Public inquiry collection, client payment, checkout, and delivery integrations are intentionally disabled.
- No paid client, real-world conversion lift, or completed customer delivery is evidenced by this repository.
- Outreach status is maintained outside this public codebase. **Do not commit prospect names, email addresses, or private conversations here.**

## Proposed offer — use the same scope everywhere

**Price:** $750 USD per proposed fixed-scope pilot, subject to individual written agreement.

**Included:** One mobile-responsive landing page; up to three service sections; an approved customer contact/inquiry route; one end-to-end authorized delivery test; two consolidated revision rounds; source files and documented handoff.

**Excluded unless separately approved:** Hosting/domain, third-party services, extra pages or integrations, branding/photo creation, long-term maintenance, paid advertising and SEO campaigns.

Do not promise more bookings, higher conversion, ranking, revenue, an unapproved delivery date, or automatically functional third-party integrations.
